# KPH40 is fun

Got Koss KPH40 utility recently and it's pretty fun. It's not as loud as I'd like but the sound quality is quite good. Pretty good detail with suprisingly decent bass. A tiny bit of sound-leak but not that bad. Sits quite comfortably on the head.

Whatever. We all know this already. That's already talked about but 1 criteria nobody mentions is headbangability. I was an IEM person for the most part because I move my head a lot when music plays. KPH40 stays on my head fine but for a violent enough head shake, it starts moving. It survives mild desk headbanging pretty well but, not as good as an IEM.

6/10 headbangable. Up next, my review on condom helicopterability.
